# The 15-second spot — "Way Out"

**Status:** script and shot list locked. Footage is AI-generated once Motion/Higgsfield access is authorized — each shot below is written as a ready-to-submit generation prompt so nothing has to be re-thought at that point.

**The idea in one line:** a calm crowd, an invisible prediction, a quiet reroute, the same calm crowd. Nobody in the ad ever notices anything — that's the pitch, dramatized.

**Tone:** warm, unhurried, documentary — not a disaster-movie trailer. No sirens, no red flashing lights, no panic. The only urgency lives in the operator's readout, briefly, and even that reads as *competence*, not alarm.

## Why this cut, not a fear-based one

The build plan's own review (`crowd_flow_review.md`) is explicit that the product's credibility rests on *not* dramatizing crush risk — crowd-safety professionals object to disaster-movie framing, and the actual differentiator ("we're 90 seconds early, so nothing dramatic ever needs to happen") is undercut by an ad that looks like a disaster warning. This script dramatizes the *absence* of a crisis instead: the visible story is relaxation on both ends, with the intervention as a two-shot detour in the middle that the crowd itself never notices. That's also the honest version of the pitch — the product's success condition is precisely that nobody in the frame ever has to feel anything.
